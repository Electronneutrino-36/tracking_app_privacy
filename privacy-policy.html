ALTAIR – Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · valid from 16 November 2026

English · Deutsch

1. Summary

  • Everything the app records stays on your phone. There is no account and no server of ours; we receive none of your data.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reports.
  • Some optional functions ask online services for help (map, place names, railway check, route estimate). Your phone then sends the coordinates or place names needed for that request directly to these services. You can switch place names and the railway check off in the app's settings.
  • Backups and PDF reports are only created when you tap the button, and only go where you choose.

3. What the app stores on your phone

To record your working day, the app stores in its private storage on your phone:

  • the positions (GPS) recorded while a working day is being recorded, with time and speed, and the drives, walks, bike and train rides derived from them;
  • start and end times, breaks, odometer readings, work categories, comments, descriptions and everything else you enter or correct;
  • looked-up place names and addresses;
  • your settings (e.g. language, colours, the name and address of your car's Bluetooth device if you choose one).

Other apps cannot read this storage. The data is not included in Android's automatic cloud backup. It stays until you delete it: a single day, "Delete all data" in the settings, or uninstalling the app.

5. Online functions

For the following functions your phone connects directly to the services named. They receive your IP address, the technical data of the request (e.g. app name, time) and the content of the request. We have no access to these requests. Each service processes the data under its own privacy policy.

Map

The map tiles are loaded from the OpenStreetMap Foundation's tile server (tile.openstreetmap.org). The request shows which map area is displayed. Privacy policy: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy. Tiles that were loaded once are kept on the phone for a while.

Place names and addresses (can be switched off)

To show towns and addresses for the start and end of each event, and to find places you type in (e.g. when adding a trip by hand), the app uses your phone's built-in geocoding service. On Android this is provided by Google, which receives the coordinates or the place names. To prefer places in your own country, the phone's last known position is also looked up this way. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Railway check (can be switched off)

To recognise train rides, the area around a ride is compared with railway lines from OpenStreetMap using the Overpass API of FOSSGIS e.V. (overpass-api.de). The request contains the coordinates of that area. Privacy: see the FOSSGIS link below.

Route estimate

Only if you tap "Estimate" for the kilometres of a trip: the coordinates of start, towns passed and destination are sent to the routing service of FOSSGIS e.V. (routing.openstreetmap.de). Privacy policy: fossgis.de (terms and privacy of the OSM servers).

Without an internet connection, or with these functions switched off, recording works fully; only names, the map background, the railway check and route estimates are missing.

6. Backups and PDF reports

When you tap "Export all days" or export a PDF report, the app creates a file and your phone asks you where to save or send it – for example on the phone, on Google Drive or by e-mail. The file then lies with the place you chose, under that provider's terms. Backup files are not encrypted and contain your routes: keep them private. Importing a backup reads the file you choose and adds its days to the app.

7. Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google processes data about the download and the installation under its own privacy policy. Google gives developers summarised statistics (e.g. number of installations per country) that do not identify you.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. As we do not receive any of your data, there is usually nothing we hold about you; you can view, correct, export and delete all data yourself in the app. For questions, write to the e-mail address above. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in Austria: Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Wien, dsb.gv.at.

9. Children

The app is meant for recording working time and is not directed at children.

10. Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy (e.g. a different map service), this page will be updated and the date above changed.

5. Online-Funktionen

Für die folgenden Funktionen verbindet sich dein Handy direkt mit den genannten Diensten. Sie erhalten deine IP-Adresse, die technischen Daten der Anfrage (z. B. App-Name, Zeitpunkt) und den Inhalt der Anfrage. Wir haben keinen Zugriff auf diese Anfragen. Jeder Dienst verarbeitet die Daten nach seiner eigenen Datenschutzerklärung.

Karte

Die Kartenkacheln werden vom Kachelserver der OpenStreetMap Foundation (tile.openstreetmap.org) geladen. Die Anfrage zeigt, welcher Kartenausschnitt angezeigt wird. Datenschutz: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy. Einmal geladene Kacheln bleiben eine Zeit lang auf dem Handy gespeichert.

Ortsnamen und Adressen (abschaltbar)

Um Orte und Adressen zu Beginn und Ende jedes Ereignisses anzuzeigen und eingetippte Orte zu finden (z. B. beim Nachtragen einer Fahrt), nutzt die App den eingebauten Geocoding-Dienst deines Handys. Unter Android stellt ihn Google bereit; Google erhält die Koordinaten bzw. die Ortsnamen. Damit Orte im eigenen Land bevorzugt werden, wird auch die zuletzt bekannte Position des Handys so nachgeschlagen. Datenschutz: policies.google.com/privacy.

Zug-Erkennung (abschaltbar)

Um Zugfahrten zu erkennen, wird das Gebiet einer Fahrt mit Bahnstrecken aus OpenStreetMap verglichen, über die Overpass-API des FOSSGIS e.V. (overpass-api.de). Die Anfrage enthält die Koordinaten dieses Gebiets. Datenschutz: siehe den FOSSGIS-Link unten.

Streckenschätzung

Nur wenn du bei einer Fahrt auf „Schätzen" tippst: Die Koordinaten von Start, durchfahrenen Orten und Ziel gehen an den Routing-Dienst des FOSSGIS e.V. (routing.openstreetmap.de). Datenschutz: fossgis.de (Nutzungsbedingungen und Datenschutz der OSM-Server).

Ohne Internet oder mit ausgeschalteten Funktionen funktioniert die Aufzeichnung vollständig; es fehlen nur Namen, der Kartenhintergrund, die Zug-Erkennung und Streckenschätzungen.
